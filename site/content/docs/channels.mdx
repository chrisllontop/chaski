---
title: Channels
description: Email, in-app and push, web and iOS. A channel is on when the config has an entry for it.
---

A channel is on when `channels` in the config has an entry for it, and each type’s template turns
it on for that type. The **Channels** page in the dashboard shows what Chaski picked up from your
config.

## Email

Email goes out through Cloudflare Email Service, from a domain you onboarded to it, through the
`send_email` binding you pass as `bindings.email`.

```ts
channels: {
  email: {
    from: 'notifications@acme.dev',
    fromName: 'Acme',
    replyTo: 'support@acme.dev',
  },
},
```

`from` must be an address the binding allows. A send can override `fromName` and `replyTo`, and
attach up to 10 files when `bindings.attachments` holds an R2 bucket. The HTML of every email carries a
pixel served from your mount, which marks the delivery opened when the message is read; that’s
why `publicUrl` must be the absolute URL of the dashboard.

A contact without an email address skips the channel with `missing_email`.

## In-app

The in-app channel keeps a feed per contact in the `ChaskiFeed` Durable Object, and pushes each new
item to their open pages over a WebSocket.

```ts
channels: {
  in_app: {},
},
```

It has no options yet: `{}` turns it on, with `bindings.feed` set. Your frontend reads the feed
through `chaski.userRoutes()`, as [Browser clients](/docs/browser-clients) shows; on the server,
`chaski.feed` lists it, counts the unread items and marks them read.

## Push

Push reaches browsers through web push and Apple devices through APNs. Configure either or both:

```ts
channels: {
  push: {
    vapid: {
      subject: 'mailto:ops@acme.dev',
      publicKey: env.VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY,
      privateKey: env.VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY,
    },
    apns: {
      teamId: '<Apple team id>',
      keyId: '<APNs key id>',
      bundleId: 'dev.acme.app',
      environment: 'production',
      privateKey: env.APNS_PRIVATE_KEY,
    },
  },
},
```

- `vapid` takes the key pair web-push tools print: the base64url public key and the private key,
  or the whole private JWK as JSON. `subject` is a `mailto:` or `https:` contact the push services
  can reach you at.
- `apns` takes your team id, the key’s id, your app’s bundle id, `sandbox` or `production`, and the
  contents of the `.p8` file.

A contact receives push on the devices registered to them. Browsers register with
`client.push.enable()` from `chaski/client`; your app can register an iOS device token from the
server:

```ts
await chaski.push.register({
  recipient: { id: user.id },
  platform: 'ios',
  deviceToken,
});
```

A contact without a registered device skips the channel with `no_device_tokens`.

<Callout title="Subscriptions belong to the key they were made with.">
  Keep your VAPID key pair once browsers subscribe: a subscription made with another public key can
  never receive this server’s pushes, and the client subscribes again when it sees one.
</Callout>
