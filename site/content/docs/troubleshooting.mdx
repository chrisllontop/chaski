---
title: Troubleshooting
description: Start from the symptom. Each one names where Chaski records the reason, and what to change.
---

## A notification did not go out

Open it under **Deliveries** in the dashboard. A channel that never sent is recorded as skipped,
with the first reason that applies:

| Reason | What to change |
| --- | --- |
| `channel_not_configured` | Save the channel’s settings on the **Channels** page. A channel with none is off. |
| `no_template` | Give the type content for that channel in its [template](/docs/templates), and turn the channel on there. |
| `muted_by_preferences` | The contact muted it, or it is muted by default. Send with `transactional: true` only when the contact must get it. |
| `missing_email` | Send the contact’s address as `recipient.email`. |
| `no_device_tokens` | Register a device for the contact before the send; see [Channels](/docs/channels#push). |

Call `ready()` from your app for the same view without the dashboard: it lists the channels that
are on, the paused ones, and the settings that stopped working under `problems`. See
[API](/docs/api#chaskientrypoint).

## A push did not arrive

- **The subscription belongs to another key.** A browser subscribed with an earlier push key can
  never receive this Chaski’s pushes. `client.push.enable()` replaces it; see
  [Browser clients](/docs/browser-clients#web-push).
- **The Apple token is in the wrong environment.** A development build’s token only works in
  `sandbox`. When APNs refuses one as `BadDeviceToken` or `DeviceTokenNotForTopic`, Chaski forgets
  the device and logs the reason to Workers Logs. Check the build’s environment and the app’s
  bundle ID in the [APNs settings](/docs/configuration#push).
- **The Windows channel expired.** WNS channels live 30 days, so the app registers its channel
  again on every launch.

## The dashboard answers “Chaski needs Cloudflare Access”

Nothing is broken. The Worker’s URL is not covered by Cloudflare Access yet, so Chaski refuses to
serve the dashboard to a request with no signed-in identity. Enable Access on the hostname from
the Worker’s **Settings** › **Domains & Routes**, not from its own **Access** tab, which refuses
the WebSocket the dashboard’s live updates use. [Install](/docs/install#turn-on-cloudflare-access)
has the steps.

## A channel shows as Failing

Its saved settings stopped working after they were saved: a binding it needs left
`wrangler.jsonc`, or `CHASKI_SECRET` changed and its credentials no longer open. Its deliveries
fail with a `config` error that says the same, and the other channels carry on. Save the setting
again on the **Channels** page. Keep `CHASKI_SECRET` as it is once credentials are saved under it.

## Deliveries wait as held

Someone paused the channel. A pause holds every delivery of that channel, including transactional
and scheduled ones, and an [inline send](/docs/sending#inline) throws `channel_paused` while its
email waits with the rest. **Resume** on the **Channels** page asks what to do with what the pause
held; kept deliveries stay under the **Held** tab of **Deliveries**. See
[Pause a channel](/docs/dashboard#pause-a-channel).

## A send fails with an unknown type

There is no template for that type, or none at all: on a fresh database, or after a deploy that
forgot to load the template file, every send is refused. Create the template in the dashboard, or
load your file with `templates().import()`. `ready()` counts the templates, which makes a health
check notice before your users do.

## Errors lose their code locally

Between two local `wrangler dev` sessions, a `ChaskiError` reaches your app as a plain `Error`
whose message starts with `ChaskiError:`, so `isChaskiError()` is `false`. Deployed Workers keep
the `name` and the `code`; write the check for the deployed shape, as
[Connect your app](/docs/connect#errors) shows.

## A local schedule stays overdue after a restart

The local Workflows engine does not wake an instance that was asleep when `wrangler dev` stopped,
so the schedule stays pending past its time. Call `schedule()` again with the same
`idempotencyKey` to restart its instance. Deployed Workflows do not have this limitation; see
[Schedules across restarts](/docs/local-development#schedules-across-restarts).

## Emails carry no open pixel

The pixel loads from where your app mounts `userRoutes()`. Set `CHASKI_PUBLIC_URL` in the Chaski
Worker’s vars to that absolute URL; without it, emails carry no pixel. If your app puts its routes
behind a session, the pixel’s path has to pass it, since an email client loads it with no session:
[Connect your app](/docs/connect#mount-the-user-routes) shows the extra mount.

## A delivery is dead

Temporary failures retry with backoff up to the queue consumer’s five attempts, then the delivery
is `dead`. Fix the cause the last error names, then replay it from **Deliveries**. Keep
`max_retries: 5` on the consumer, as [Install](/docs/install) declares it: Chaski counts on it to
tell a delivery’s last attempt.
