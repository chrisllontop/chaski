title: Introduction
description: Chaski is notification infrastructure that runs inside your Cloudflare Worker, dashboard included.
---

Chaski lives inside your Worker. You mount it on a route of your app, and it runs on your own
Cloudflare resources: it deploys with your Worker, and your data stays in your account. It’s a
library you mount, not another service.

## What it does

- **Email, push and in-app.** Email through Cloudflare Email Service, web push, push to iOS through
  APNs, and an in-app feed that updates live.
- **Templates** for the content of every notification, written in Liquid and edited in the
  dashboard with a live preview.
- **Preferences** that let each contact mute the notifications they don’t want.
- **Scheduling** for notifications that go out later, with cancellation.
- **Retries** for deliveries that fail along the way, and a replay for the ones that run out.
- **A dashboard**, served from the same route, behind the sign-in you choose.

## How it fits together

Your Worker declares the bindings Chaski uses and hands them over in a config function. A send
records the notification in your database, then puts one delivery per channel on your queue. Your
Worker’s queue consumer passes those batches back to Chaski, which renders each template and hands
it to the provider, retrying what fails.

```ts title="src/billing.ts"
await chaski.send({
  type: 'payment_received',
  recipient: { id: user.id, email: user.email },
  payload: { amount: '$15.00' },
});
```

Three entry points serve the rest:

- `chaski.routes()` mounts the dashboard and its API, for your team.
- `chaski.userRoutes()` mounts the endpoints your frontend calls for the signed-in user: their
  feed, their devices and their preferences.
- `ChaskiEntrypoint` gives your other Workers the same API over RPC.
