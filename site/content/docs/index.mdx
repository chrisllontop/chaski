Chaski sends email, push and an in-app feed for your app, from a Worker of its own on your
Cloudflare account. The full list of what it covers is on [chaski.sh](https://chaski.sh). This
page is the shape of it, and where each part is documented.

## How it fits together

The Chaski Worker holds the database, the queue and everything else Chaski runs on. Your app’s
Worker binds to it, one Chaski per environment, and a send is one call:

```ts title="src/billing.ts"
await env.CHASKI.send({
  type: 'payment_received',
  recipient: { id: user.id, email: user.email },
  payload: { amount: '$15.00' },
});
```

Chaski records the notification, then puts one delivery per channel on its queue. Its own consumer
renders each template and hands it to the provider, retrying what fails.

Three surfaces serve the rest:

- **The dashboard**, at the Chaski Worker’s URL, for your team.
- **`ChaskiEntrypoint`**, the API your Workers call over a service binding: sends, schedules,
  feeds, preferences, devices and templates.
- **`userRoutes()`**, which your app mounts for its frontend: the signed-in user’s feed, devices
  and preferences, forwarded to Chaski for the user your app says is signed in.
