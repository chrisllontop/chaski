## Where it lives

The dashboard and its API are the Chaski Worker’s own URL, such as
`https://chaski.<your-subdomain>.workers.dev`. It ships built inside the package, so there is
nothing to upload or host: deploying the Worker deploys it.

## Who signs in

Cloudflare Access decides who gets in. Access signs people in before their request reaches the
Worker, and hands Chaski who they are; Chaski opens the dashboard only to a request that carries
that identity, and answers **Chaski needs Cloudflare Access** to any other. There’s no password or
sign-in of Chaski’s own to configure. [Install](/docs/install#turn-on-cloudflare-access) turns
Access on.

Everyone Access lets in can do everything: edit templates, replay deliveries, change channel
settings and pause channels. Choose who that is in the Access application’s policy, such as your
team’s email domain or a group of your identity provider. Each change is recorded under the email
Access signed in.

Under `wrangler dev`, Wrangler signs in the identity under `access.dev` in `wrangler.jsonc`, as
Access would; see [Local development](/docs/local-development#run-chaski).

## What it shows

- **Overview**: notifications, sent, success rate and open rate for a period, deliveries per day,
  delivery latency and the health of each type on each channel.
- **Deliveries**: every delivery and its status, with each attempt’s error, a replay for the
  failed and dead ones, and the held ones a paused channel keeps.
- **Schedules**: pending scheduled sends, which you can cancel. One still pending a minute after
  its time shows as overdue.
- **Templates**: the editor, with a live preview and a test send for email.
- **Channels**: each channel’s settings, and a pause for each channel that runs.

## Pause a channel

**Pause** on the **Channels** page is the emergency brake: a provider failing, a bug sending the
same push again and again, content that went out wrong. From then on every delivery of that
channel waits as **held** instead of going out, transactional and scheduled ones included, and
the header shows the channel as paused. The pause lives in Chaski’s database, so it stays until
someone resumes the channel, deploys included.

**Resume** asks what to do with what the pause held: send it all now, or keep it. Kept deliveries
stay under the **Held** tab of **Deliveries**, where you send or discard each one, or every one of
a channel and type. A delivery sends only once its channel runs again, and a discarded one shows as
skipped and never goes out. An [inline send](/docs/sending#inline) on a paused channel throws
`channel_paused`, and its email waits as held like the rest.

Pausing holds deliveries; removing a channel’s settings turns the channel off, and its sends skip
it without holding anything. `ready()` lists the paused channels under `paused`, for a health
check that should notice.

## Live updates

With the `ChaskiEvents` Durable Object bound as `CHASKI_EVENTS`, open dashboards refresh as
deliveries change, and the header says **Live**. Without it, the dashboard still works, and
refreshes when you come back to its tab or reload it.
