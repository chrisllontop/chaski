---
title: Your first notification
description: Create a local Chaski, give payment_received a template, send it from your app, and follow the delivery in the dashboard.
---

This guide sends one notification on your machine. Nothing reaches your Cloudflare account and no
email reaches an inbox: the local dashboard shows the delivery, and the email lands on disk.
Deploy when it works here, with [Install](/docs/install).

## Create and run Chaski [step]

```npm
npx chaskijs@next init
```

Run it in the Chaski project it creates:

```bash
npx wrangler dev
```

The dashboard is at `http://localhost:8787`, signed in as the identity under `access.dev` in
`wrangler.jsonc`. [Local development](/docs/local-development) has the details.

## Turn on two channels [step]

Open **Channels** in the local dashboard. Every channel starts off, so save a sender address for
**Email** and turn **In-app** on. A channel with no saved settings is skipped on every send, with
the reason recorded on the delivery.

## Create the template [step]

Under **Templates**, create one for the type `payment_received` and turn on email and in-app.
Write what a person reads, with the values your app will send:

```liquid title="Email subject"
Payment of {{ amount }} received
```

```liquid title="Email body"
<p>Thanks, {{ recipient.name }}. We received your payment of {{ amount }}.</p>
```

Give the in-app content a title and a message of the same kind, save sample data with the
template, and check the preview. [Templates](/docs/templates) covers languages and formatting.

## Connect your app [step]

[Connect your app](/docs/connect) sets this up in full. The short version: bind your app’s Worker
to the local Chaski by its Worker name, mount the user routes, then run your app on another port
while Chaski runs:

```bash
npx wrangler dev --port 8788
```

A service binding finds a Worker another `wrangler dev` runs on the same machine by name, so your
app reaches the local Chaski as it will reach the deployed one.

## Send it [step]

Send from a route you can call, such as the handler that records a payment:

```ts title="src/billing.ts"
await env.CHASKI.send({
  type: 'payment_received',
  recipient: { id: user.id, email: user.email, name: user.name },
  payload: { amount: '$15.00' },
  idempotencyKey: `payment:${payment.id}`,
});
```

Call the route, then open **Deliveries** in the local dashboard and follow each delivery from
`pending` to `sent`. [Read the emails](/docs/local-development#read-the-emails) shows where the
local email went. A delivery that shows as skipped names its reason; when one does not go out at
all, [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting) starts from that reason.

## What to do next

- [Install](/docs/install) deploys this Chaski to your account and puts its dashboard behind
  Cloudflare Access.
- [Preferences](/docs/preferences) gives your users a settings screen for what they receive.
- [Scheduling](/docs/scheduling) sends the reminder before the invoice is due, and cancels it
  when they pay.
